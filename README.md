## TL;DR

### The Problem
Alen Sultanic's **Nothing Held Back Plus (NHB+)** is widely regarded as one of the most comprehensive archives on direct-response customer acquisition, offer mechanics, financial economics, and high-ticket sales ever assembled. But in its raw state across MEGA, it spanned **320 GB of loose video and audio files** across nearly 4 years. Finding a specific breakdown—whether it was the *5-Step Sales Process*, *Thoughts Into Emotions*, or *Competing on Economics*—required manually scrubbing through hundreds of 3-hour MP4s.

### The Solution
This repository contains the **complete verbatim transcription and indexing of the entire NHB+ archive** from June 2021 through February 2025. Every call, hot-seat coaching critique, guest masterclass, and bonus curriculum is fully transcribed, speaker-diarized, chronologically sorted, and indexed for sub-second search in Obsidian or programmatic querying by AI agents.

### Why This Corpus?

| Dimension | The Raw Archive | This Corpus |
|---|---|---|

## Troubleshooting

### 1. "Obsidian is taking a long time to index the vault"
- **Cause:** Obsidian indexes all 273 markdown notes and 6.95 million words upon first launch.
- **Fix:** Allow 15–30 seconds for Obsidian's background cache to complete indexing. Once indexed, search operations will be instantaneous.

### 2. "Wikilinks in `00 Topics.md` don't open the target note"
- **Cause:** Obsidian's internal link resolution settings might be set to *Relative path only*.
- **Fix:** In Obsidian Settings → *Files and links* → set *New link format* to *Shortest path when possible* or *Relative path to file*.

### 3. "Attachments won't open directly from markdown notes"
- **Cause:** Native operating system file associations for `.docx`, `.xlsx`, or `.pptx` are missing.
- **Fix:** Ensure you have Microsoft Office, LibreOffice, or compatible readers installed. Attachments reside in `vault/attachments/`.

### 4. "Agent exceeds context window when analyzing the whole corpus"
- **Cause:** Prompting an AI agent to ingest all 6.95 million words in a single context window will exceed model token limits.
- **Fix:** Have your agent consult `vault/00 Topics.md` first to identify target dates and topics, or scope queries to specific directories (e.g. `vault/calls/2024/` or `vault/courses/`).

## Scope & Design Boundaries

- **Verbatim Source Truth:** Notes contain the raw, verbatim spoken words of Alen Sultanic, guest speakers, and attendees. They have intentionally **not** been summarized or compressed by an LLM so that the unfiltered teaching, phrasing, and tactical nuances remain completely intact.
- **Speaker Attribution:** Deepgram Nova-3 accurately separates turns of speech by speaker (`Speaker 0`, `Speaker 1`, etc.), but does not infer the legal identities of unnamed attendee questioners in open hot-seat calls.
- **Native Companion Binaries:** Original companion files (`.docx`, `.xlsx`, `.pdf`, `.pptx`) are preserved as native binary files in `vault/attachments/` rather than converted into lossy markdown.
